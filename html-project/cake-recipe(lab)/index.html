cake recipe

This easy homemade vanilla cake needs simple ingredients like flour, sugar, and eggs

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 4 large eggs (at room temperature)
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup milk
  • 3 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3 teaspoons vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Turn on your oven to 180°C (350°F) and grease a round cake pan.
  2. Mix the flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  3. Beat the eggs and sugar with a mixer until thick and fluffy.
  4. Warm the milk and butter together until the butter melts.
  5. Gently stir the flour into the egg mix.
  6. Pour in the warm milk, oil, and vanilla, then mix gently.
  7. Pour the batter into your pan.
  8. Bake for 30 minutes until a toothpick put into the middle comes out clean.
  9. Let the cake cool before eating.
