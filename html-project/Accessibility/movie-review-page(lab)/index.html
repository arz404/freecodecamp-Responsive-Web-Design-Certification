Movie Review: Rise Beyond

Breaking Bad is a hit American television crime drama created by Vince Gilligan. It follows Walter White, a poor high school chemistry teacher in New Mexico who gets terminal lung cancer. He starts making and selling pure blue crystal meth with a former student to pay his medical bills and secure his family's money.
